Effective Date: [ October 5, 2026]

At Mom's Kitchen, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us when you contact us or place an order.

When you contact Mom's Kitchen through WhatsApp, phone, social media or other communication channels, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name

  • Delivery or pickup location

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

Mom's Kitchen uses WhatsApp as a primary method for receiving food-order enquiries and communicating with customers. When you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your communication is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policies and terms.

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary to process your order, arrange delivery, provide customer support, or comply with applicable law.

Our website may contain links or services provided by third parties, including WhatsApp and social media platforms. Their use of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

7. Your Choices

If you have questions about information you have shared with Mom's Kitchen, you may contact us using the details provided on our website.
