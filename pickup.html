Pickup Policy

Effective Date: [ADD DATE]

Mom's Kitchen currently operates on a pickup-only basis. Customers can place their food order in advance and collect their confirmed order from our designated pickup location at the agreed time.

1. Pickup Only

Mom's Kitchen does not currently provide home delivery. Customers are required to collect their confirmed orders from the pickup location provided by Mom's Kitchen.

2. Order Confirmation

Orders are placed and confirmed through WhatsApp. Sending an order enquiry does not automatically mean that the order has been accepted.

An order will be considered confirmed only after Mom's Kitchen confirms the food items, quantity, price and pickup time with the customer.

3. Pickup Time

Customers should arrive at the agreed pickup time so that the order can be collected while the food is fresh and ready.

If you expect to be late, please contact Mom's Kitchen through WhatsApp as soon as possible.

4. Pickup Location

The pickup address and any necessary pickup instructions will be provided to the customer after the order has been confirmed.

5. Bulk Orders

Large and bulk orders may require advance notice so that sufficient preparation time can be arranged.

Customers placing bulk orders should contact Mom's Kitchen in advance and confirm the required quantity and pickup time.

6. Changes to Pickup Time

If you need to change your pickup time, please contact Mom's Kitchen as early as possible.

Changes to the pickup time are subject to preparation availability.

7. Customer Responsibility

Customers are responsible for collecting their order from the agreed pickup location at the confirmed time.

Customers should also ensure that the pickup information provided during the ordering process is correct.

8. Food Collection

Customers are encouraged to collect their food as close as possible to the confirmed pickup time.

Once the order has been handed over to the customer, responsibility for transporting and storing the food rests with the customer.

9. Pickup Instructions

Any specific instructions regarding the pickup location, entrance, contact person or collection process will be communicated through WhatsApp when the order is confirmed.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about your pickup, please contact Mom's Kitchen before arriving.
