MOM'S KITCHEN

Food & Allergy Information

Mom's Kitchen prepares homemade food in a kitchen environment. We take care in preparing our food, but customers with food allergies or dietary restrictions should contact us before placing an order.

1. Allergies

Please inform us about any known food allergies before confirming your order.

2. Ingredients

Customers may ask about ingredients used in a particular dish before ordering.

3. Cross-Contact

Because food may be prepared in a shared kitchen environment, we cannot guarantee that a food item is completely free from contact with all possible allergens.

4. Dietary Requirements

Please inform us in advance about any dietary requirements or restrictions so that we can discuss available options.

5. Questions

If you have questions about ingredients or preparation, please contact Mom's Kitchen before placing your order.
